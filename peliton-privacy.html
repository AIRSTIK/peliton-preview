Privacy & Cookie Policy

How Peliton Plastics handles the information you share with us.

Information We Collect

When you contact us through our quote form or by email, we collect the details you choose to provide — such as your name, company, email address, phone number, and a description of your project. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and to prepare your quote.

How We Use It

We use your information to answer your questions, provide quotations, and fulfill your order. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Project details you share are treated as confidential, and we are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement on request.

Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies and similar technologies to help the site function and to understand general traffic patterns. You can set your browser to refuse cookies; the site will still work, though some conveniences may be affected.

Contact Us

To ask a question about this policy, or to request that we update or remove your information, call us at (229) 247-1269 or email robert@peliton.com.
