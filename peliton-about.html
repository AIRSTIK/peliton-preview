Our Story

Hard Work, Dedication & Made in America.

For nearly three decades, Peliton Plastics has turned ideas into finished products — one carefully made part at a time, right here in South Georgia.

Since 1996

From One Machine to a 35,000 Sq. Ft. Facility.

Peliton was founded in 1996 by Robert McKinnon. We began with one old plastic injection molding machine in a 3,000 sq. ft. rented building. Thanks to our team's dedication to customer satisfaction, we now own a 35,000 sq. ft. facility with state-of-the-art molding machines.

Today, Peliton is a full-service plastic injection molding and manufacturing company. We offer molding, mold making, assembly, packaging, machining, ultrasonic welding, printing and shipping — all at the lowest possible cost. Our philosophy is simple: when your project is a profitable success, our company is a success.

We start with your idea and turn it into a finished, working product that fully meets your expectations and specifications.

What's in a Name?

Peliton = Peloton.

"Peloton" is a term from bicycle racing — a team of highly motivated riders working together, drafting in close formation to power through the wind toward the finish line.

In manufacturing, it's our team working closely with your team — overcoming any adversity to bring your product to market. That idea is baked into our name, and into how we work with every customer.

Looking Ahead

Our Vision for the Future.

We continue to grow and expand our capabilities to meet our customers' needs. We've recently added a 650-ton Dima molding machine, a larger and faster CNC, and a climate-controlled room for our pad printing — and we've expanded the machine shop to make room for what's next.
