Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Peliton Plastics do?

We're a full-service custom plastic injection molding and contract manufacturing company in Valdosta, GA. We mold, assemble, machine, and print parts entirely in the USA — with in-house mold making, ultrasonic welding, pad printing and screen printing.

Do you handle low-volume runs and prototypes?

Absolutely. With machines from 55 to 650 tons and shot sizes from 0.001 to 5 pounds, we run both short and long jobs — plus 3D-printed prototypes to prove out your design first.

Will you sign an NDA for my invention?

Yes. We're inventor-friendly and happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement so your idea stays protected. Bring CAD files, a sketch, or even a cardboard cut-out.

Can you help me move production back to the USA?

Yes — reshoring and re-tooling is one of our specialties. We'll help you weigh cost, speed, communication and IP security, then make the switch smooth.

How do I get a quote?

Call (229) 247-1269 or send your details through our contact page. A quotation is only a phone call away, and there's never any obligation.
