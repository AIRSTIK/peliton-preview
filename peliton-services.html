Capabilities

Everything Your Part Needs, Under One Roof.

Molding, tooling, machining, assembly and printing — all performed in-house in Valdosta, GA. One vendor, one point of contact, one location from raw resin to finished, packaged product.

01 — CORE SERVICE

Custom Plastic Injection Molding

From a single idea to full production. We mold a wide variety of thermoplastics and engineered resins to your exact specifications, large parts or small, long runs or short.

  • Machines from 55 to 650 tons of clamping force
  • Shot sizes from 0.001 to 5.00 pounds
  • Insert molding & overmolding
  • High- and low-volume production
02 — IN-HOUSE TOOLING

Mold Making, Repair & Machine Shop

Our machine shop was built to support our own molding — which means your tooling never waits on an outside vendor. We also repair molds for other manufacturers across the Southeast.

  • Mold building, repair & maintenance
  • CNC milling, lathes, surface grinding
  • Sinker EDM & mold texturing
  • Mastercam CAD/CAM programming
03 — FINISHING

Assembly, Welding & Fulfillment

We take parts molded here, add any second operations, combine them with outside components, then package and ship a finished product to your door.

  • Ultrasonic welding (Branson)
  • Kitting & sub-assembly
  • Automated sleeve wrap & packaging
  • Shipping & customer fulfillment
04 — DECORATION

Pad Printing & Screen Printing

No need to ship your product to a second location to be printed — our state-of-the-art printing department does it right here, on virtually any surface.

  • Pad printing on 3-D & recessed parts
  • Screen printing on plastic, vinyl, metal, glass, wood
  • Logos, placards & promotional products
  • In-house graphic design
05 — FROM IDEA TO PART

3D Prototyping & Project Management

Prove out your design before you commit to tooling. We turn CAD — or a sketch — into a 3D-printed prototype, then manage your whole project through our network of trusted tool builders.

  • 3D printing (Bambu Lab X1C)
  • Design-for-manufacture guidance
  • Full project coordination
  • Local tool builders within driving range
