In-House Decoration

Pad & Screen Printing, Under the Same Roof.

No need to ship your product to a second location to be printed. Our state-of-the-art printing department decorates plastics, signage and promotional products in-house — with quality workmanship and fast turnaround.

SCREEN PRINTING

High-Volume Screen Printing

From a single logo to long production runs, we screen print on virtually any flat surface and can create original artwork or duplicate your existing signs.

  • Vinyl, Coroplast, metal, glass & wood
  • Real estate, political & campaign signs
  • Promotional products, placards & auto tags
  • In-house graphic design
PAD PRINTING

Pad Printing on 3-D & Recessed Parts

Pad printing reaches the shapes screen printing can't — irregular, recessed and three-dimensional products. We run both large and small order quantities in a climate-controlled room for consistent results.

  • Prints curved, recessed & textured surfaces
  • Large & small order quantities
  • Climate-controlled for color consistency
  • Pairs perfectly with our molded parts
