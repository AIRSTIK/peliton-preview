Growing Without Cessation

News & Milestones.

Awards, machine arrivals and shop expansions. A snapshot of how Peliton keeps investing in American manufacturing.

New Equipment

650-Ton Dima Machine Arrives

The long-awaited Dima 650 — a 38,000-pound, semi-trailer-sized press — was set in place in under three hours with the help of a 28-ton hydraulic crane. It gives us greater capacity and the ability to mold much larger parts.

Recognition

Small Business of the Year

Peliton received the Small Business of the Year Award for its service to persons with disabilities, presented by Valdosta Mayor John J. Fretti. Accepting were president Robert McKinnon, sales manager Ed Musser and operations manager Mike Jones.

In the News

Record Campaign-Sign Year & Fox 31 Feature

Our screen-printing department reported a record year for political and campaign signs — and WFXL Fox 31 interviewed sales manager Ed Musser about it. Thank you to every customer who recommended us.

Community

Governor's Honors Program Tour

We were proud to host Georgia's Governor's Honors Program — more than 30 gifted and talented high-school students toured the facility, asked questions and got hands-on manufacturing experience.

Expansion

Climate-Controlled Pad-Printing Room

To deliver the highest-quality pad printing, we built a climate-controlled room held at 73°F and 30% humidity in a relatively dust-free environment for our printing and plate-making.

New Equipment

More Presses, More Capacity

A run of new machines joined the floor: a 230-Ton and 170-Ton Van Dorn Demag, a 120-Ton Van Dorn, and a Sumitomo SH75A 75-Ton — expanding our mid- and small-part capacity. We also added a larger, faster CNC to the mold shop.

Quality

State-of-the-Art Moisture Analyzer

Knowing the moisture content of raw material is vital to top-quality parts, so we added a Computrac MAX 4000XL Moisture Analyzer — staying current on the technology that ensures customer satisfaction.

Convenience

We Now Accept All Major Credit Cards

Because you asked for it — Peliton now accepts all major credit cards through PayPal, a secure electronic alternative to checks and money orders.

Let's Build Something

Whether you're reshoring production or launching a new product, we'd love to help.
