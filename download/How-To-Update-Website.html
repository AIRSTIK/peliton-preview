How to Put Your New Website Online

A simple, step-by-step guide. Takes about 15 minutes. No tech skills needed.

Your new website is one file. You will download it, then upload it to where your website lives, and that's it. Just do the steps in order, from 1 to 8. You can't break anything — Step 4 makes a backup first.

Before you start, you need two things

1. Your website loginThis is the login for your hosting company — the company you pay to keep peliton.com online. The screen you need is called “File Manager” (sometimes “cPanel”).

Don't know it? Call your hosting company and say: “I need to log in to File Manager for peliton.com.”
2. A computer with internetA regular Windows or Mac computer. (Not a phone — uploading is much easier on a computer.)

Get the new website

⬇ Click here to download the new website https://github.com/AIRSTIK/peliton-preview/raw/main/download/peliton-website-files.zip

This saves one file called peliton-website-files.zip to your Downloads folder. Do not open it or change it. The pictures are already inside — you don't need anything else.

The 8 steps

  1. Download the new website.
    Click the green button above. A file named peliton-website-files.zip goes into your Downloads. Leave it as-is.
  2. Log in to your File Manager.
    Go to your hosting company's website and log in. Click the button called File Manager. (Not sure where it is? Call your hosting company and ask them to get you to “File Manager for peliton.com.”)
  3. Open the public_html folder.
    This is the folder where your website lives. Double-click it to open it. (On a few hosts it's called www or htdocs instead — same thing.)
  4. Make a backup first. SAFETY — don't skip
    Inside that folder, select everything (look for Select All). Click Compress → make a .zip → then download that zip to your computer and keep it somewhere safe. This is your “undo” button. Now nothing can be lost.
  5. Upload the new website.
    Click the Upload button. Choose the peliton-website-files.zip you downloaded in Step 1. Wait until it says 100% / Complete, then go back to the public_html folder.
  6. Unzip it on the server.
    Right-click the peliton-website-files.zip you just uploaded and choose Extract. If it asks “Overwrite?” click Yes — that's what replaces the old site with the new one. When it's done, you can delete the leftover .zip to keep things tidy.
    No “Extract” button on your screen? No problem. On your own computer, right-click the downloaded zip → Extract All to get 12 files. Then in Step 5 upload all 12 files instead of the zip, and click Yes/Overwrite if asked. Skip this step 6.
  7. Look at your new website.
    Go to peliton.com. Hold Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) to force a fresh load. The new design should appear.
  8. Check it on your phone too.
    Open peliton.com on your phone. It's built to look great on phones, tablets, and computers.
Changed your mind, or something looks off? You can put the old site back anytime. Just upload the backup zip you made in Step 4 the same way, extract it, and overwrite. Your old website is back. Nothing here is permanent.
